Local spell checker for French and English on macOS

Many Mac users write in more than one language. A local spell checker with LLM support can help with English, French, short translations, and grammar cleanup without relying on a cloud writing editor.

Why multilingual correction is hard

Traditional spell checkers can catch spelling errors, but multilingual writing often needs tone, grammar, idiom, and context. Local LLMs can help when the selected model handles both languages well.

Useful searches Qelvora targets

Qelvora is relevant for searches such as local spell checker, correcteur orthographique local, local grammar checker, and local LLM translation on Mac. The core idea is the same: use local models for selected text.

Model choice matters

Some local models are better at English, others are stronger in multilingual tasks. Test your real writing, not only benchmarks. Check names, product terms, accents, and professional tone.

Quick takeaway

Qelvora can support French and English correction workflows on macOS when paired with a capable local Ollama model and careful human review.

Practical checklist

For local spell checking, start with a short selection rather than a whole document. Ask for correction, clarity, or translation as a narrow task. Then compare the result with the original sentence and make sure the model preserved names, numbers, dates, product terms, and the writer's intent.

This habit matters for SEO, support, product, and developer writing because the best output is not the most rewritten output. The best output is the version that is clearer while still being true to the original context.
