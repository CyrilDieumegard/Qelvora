Privacy guide

Private AI writing assistant for macOS

Qelvora is built for people who want AI writing correction without moving every draft into a hosted editor. It runs as a native macOS menu bar utility and sends selected text to local Ollama models on your machine.

What problem does Qelvora solve?

Most writing assistants expect you to paste text into a web app or send it to a remote API. That can be fine for public copy, but it is uncomfortable for customer replies, investor updates, support notes, bug reports, internal planning, and anything that should remain close to your device.

Qelvora focuses on the narrow daily workflow: select text in a Mac app, trigger the correction action, review the result, and copy the polished version back where you were already working.

Why local models matter

Local inference gives you more control over where text is processed. With Ollama installed, Qelvora can use model families such as Gemma, Qwen, DeepSeek, Kimi, Mistral, Llama, or Phi as local writing engines. The best model depends on your hardware, language, and preferred writing style.

Who should use it?

Qelvora is useful for founders, developers, support teams, privacy-first operators, and independent builders who write inside many different macOS apps. It is especially relevant when the goal is fast correction, rewriting, or translation rather than long-form document generation.

Direct answer

Qelvora is a private AI writing assistant for macOS that corrects, rewrites, and translates selected text with local Ollama models instead of a cloud writing service.
