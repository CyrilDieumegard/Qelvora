Discord-first screen text

Draw a rectangle. Correct or translate the words inside.

Discord's composer and message history do not always behave like a document editor, especially when you need one exact sentence or a quick translation. Built-in selection, spelling, and translation behavior also varies by app, surface, language, and context. Press Qelvora's global shortcut and a precise rectangle follows your mouse.

  1. 01
    Press the shortcut

    Qelvora immediately opens the screen-area selector over the app in front of you.

  2. 02
    Draw the rectangle

    Drag around exactly the draft, message, code comment, or visible words you need.

  3. 03
    Process it locally

    Local OCR reads the area, then your Ollama model corrects or translates the recognized text.

Prefer normal selection? Highlight editable text and use Correct selection from Qelvora's menu bar or macOS Services. Both modes remain available.

FAQ

Straight answers before you install.

What is Qelvora?

Qelvora is a private local AI writing assistant and local spell checker for macOS. It corrects, rewrites, and translates selected text using Ollama models running on your Mac.

Is Qelvora a local grammar checker?

Yes. Qelvora can act as a local grammar checker and local LLM corrector for selected text, using installed Ollama model tags instead of a hosted writing service.

Does Qelvora send text to the cloud?

Qelvora is built around local model inference. The product is designed for workflows where drafts, customer replies, or internal notes should stay on the machine.

Which models work with Qelvora?

Qelvora uses installed Ollama model tags. Local writing workflows can use model families such as Gemma, Qwen, DeepSeek, Kimi, Mistral, Llama, and Phi.

Can Qelvora correct or translate text in Discord with OCR?

Yes. When normal text selection is awkward in Discord, draw a rectangle around visible text. Qelvora runs OCR locally on the Mac, then uses the configured local Ollama model to correct or translate the recognized text. Native selection, spell-check, and translation behavior can vary by surface and context.

Who is Qelvora for?

Qelvora is useful for founders, developers, support teams, and privacy-first operators who need fast writing cleanup inside normal macOS apps.
